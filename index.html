My sweet boy,

Six months ago, on May 4, I got to call you my boyfriend. And even now, seeing your name light up my phone makes me smile like an absolute idiot. I love that, somehow, after all these months, you can still make me feel like the boy who first fell for you.

There have been miles between us, busy days, FaceTimes that I never want to end, and goodbyes where I already start counting down until the next hello. But there have also been so many wonderful moments: our time together in Richmond, our silly conversations, our kisses and cuddles, and getting to experience Disney and Epcot with you. I keep collecting all of these moments in my head because they feel so much more special with you in them.

I love your smile. I love your laugh. I love how handsome you are even when you aren't trying. I love getting to be soft and a little ridiculous around you. And, yes, I love being your hopelessly affectionate, occasionally dramatic, completely smitten boyfriend.

I don't need us to have every moment perfectly figured out. I just want us to keep showing up, learning each other, making memories, and finding new reasons to smile together. Six months is only the beginning of what I hope is a very, very long story.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for letting me love you. And thank you for being the person I get to call mine.

Happy six months, babe. I love you. ♡
Forever your boy,
